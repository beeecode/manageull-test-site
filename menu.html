Our Menu

Our menu changes with the seasons and the harvest, but a few things stay constant: coffee roasted in-house within the last two weeks, milk from a dairy twenty minutes upriver, and pastries baked fresh each morning by our neighbors at Ashford Falls Bakehouse.

Coffee & Espresso Drinks

  • Willow River Pour-Over

    Our current single-origin light roast, brewed fresh cup by cup for a bright, tea-like finish.

  • Riverside Espresso

    A house blend of Colombian and Sumatran beans, pulled short for a rich, chocolatey shot.

  • Cedar Smoke Latte

    Espresso with steamed oat milk and a whisper of housemade cedar-smoked syrup.

  • Nitro Cold Brew

    Steeped for eighteen hours and poured on nitro for a naturally sweet, creamy finish, no sugar needed.

Bakery & Food

  • Buttermilk Cardamom Scone

    Baked daily by Ashford Falls Bakehouse and served warm with honey butter on the side.

  • Smoked Salmon Bagel

    Toasted everything bagel with herbed cream cheese, cucumber, and river-smoked salmon.
